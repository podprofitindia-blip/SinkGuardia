# SinkGuardia Affiliate Website — Starter

## What is included
- Responsive English homepage
- 5 researched starter product cards
- Product search
- Affiliate disclosure
- About and Contact pages
- Placeholder affiliate links

## Before publishing
1. Replace YOUR_AFFILIATE_LINK_1 ... YOUR_AFFILIATE_LINK_5 in `js/products.js`.
2. Replace YOUR_EMAIL@example.com in `contact.html`.
3. Replace placeholder product-image areas with images you are legally allowed to use.
4. Verify each product's current price, rating and specifications.
5. Add your original review/buying-guide articles.
6. Apply to your chosen affiliate program and follow its current terms.

## Local preview
Open `index.html` directly in a browser, or run a simple local server:
`python -m http.server 8000`
Then open `http://localhost:8000`.

## Important
The displayed prices/ratings are research snapshots and can change. They are not guarantees.
